## Analysis structure

1. Define the healthcare capacity or coordination problem and population affected.
2. Describe the civilian and military responsibilities without assuming common legal authority.
3. Map the process: recognition, request, approval, coordination, delivery, handover and feedback.
4. Identify interoperability requirements: terminology, documentation, communication, competencies and responsibilities.
5. Separate documented gaps, inference and unanswered questions.
6. Propose options with owners, resources, constraints and measurable outcomes.
7. Trace one public example from observation to decision, implementation and evaluation.

## Example questions

- Who may request support, approve it and accept responsibility?
- What information is needed, by whom, and through which approved channel?
- Which competencies are required for the stated tasks?
- How are civilian service continuity, reciprocity and workforce effects considered?
- What evidence would demonstrate improved capability rather than an activity count?

Use published references and non-sensitive examples. This template does not claim NATO endorsement or compliance with any particular STANAG or AMedP edition.
