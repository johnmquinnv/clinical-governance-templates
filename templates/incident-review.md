**Version:** 1.1.0 · **Last editorial update:** 22 October 2026  
**Maintainer:** John Quinn, MD, MPH, PhD, EMT-P. Educational draft; see the [repository review status](../README.md).

A blank, systems-focused structure for a learning review. The aim is learning and improvement, not blame. Follow your local incident-reporting policy and legal duties (for example duty of candour, regulator notification).

In England, the NHS Patient Safety Incident Response Framework (PSIRF) promotes a system-based approach to learning from incidents. Adapt this template to the framework used in your setting.

## 6. Analysis
Use a proportionate systems-based learning response. In an NHS England PSIRF setting, use the national learning-response tools or a suitable systems-based equivalent, such as SEIPS (Systems Engineering Initiative for Patient Safety).

Explore interactions between people, tasks, tools and technology, the physical environment and organisational conditions. Compare the intended process with how work is actually done, using interviews, observations and timeline or process mapping as appropriate.

Do not rely on a linear "five whys" exercise or a single presumed root cause as the sole analysis. The purpose is to understand the interacting conditions that shaped the event and identify feasible improvements.
