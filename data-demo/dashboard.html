Read the measures

Reviewed and handover completeness use all displayed records. Action closure includes only records requiring an action. Open overdue counts required actions that remain open and are flagged overdue in this fictional snapshot.

These are documentation measures. They do not establish clinical safety, patient outcomes or a ranking of teams. No traffic-light thresholds are applied.
